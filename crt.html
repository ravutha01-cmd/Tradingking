Train the strategy

Tries every combination of the CRT rules (range size, sweep depth, close depth, trend filter, target, stop buffer, minimum reward:risk, holding time). Settings are chosen only on the older data, scored on how consistently they profit across 4 slices of it. They are then tested on the newer data they never saw. Only the test result tells you how the strategy might do going forward.
